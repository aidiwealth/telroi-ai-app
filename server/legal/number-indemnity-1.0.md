# Telroi Number Use Indemnity

**Version 1.0** · Effective [DATE]

This Number Use Indemnity (the "Indemnity") applies where you request a Telroi
number for one or more of the sensitive call categories in section 2. It is
additional to the Telroi Terms of Use and does not replace them. Where the two
conflict on the subject matter below, this Indemnity governs.

In this document, "Telroi" means the Telroi entity that provisions the number to
you, "you" means the account holder, and "the Number" means each number
provisioned under this Indemnity.

---

## 1. When this applies

**1.1** Telroi will not provision or enable a number for a sensitive call
category until this Indemnity has been accepted by a person authorised to bind
you.

**1.2** Acceptance is recorded against the specific number, the accepting user
and the version of this document in force at that time. A copy is available to
you at any time from your workspace.

**1.3** If you begin using an existing number for a sensitive call category
after it was provisioned, you must accept this Indemnity for that number before
doing so. Telroi may suspend a number used for such traffic without a current
acceptance on file.

---

## 2. Sensitive call categories

This Indemnity applies to any number used for:

- **Authentication** — one-time passcodes, verification calls, transaction
  alerts and any traffic used to confirm identity or authorise an action.
- **Financial services** — banking, payments, lending, collections, debt
  recovery, insurance and other regulated financial communications.
- **Health and sensitive data** — health, medical, insurance and any traffic
  involving special-category or sensitive personal data.
- **Emergency and welfare** — emergency, safety, crisis, welfare and helpline
  services.
- **Government and public sector** — government, public authority, civic and
  electoral communications.
- **Scaled outbound** — outbound campaigns at volume, and any automated,
  predictive or AI-initiated dialling.

If you are unsure whether your intended use falls within a category, ask us
before you begin. Proceeding without asking does not put the traffic outside
this Indemnity.

## 3. What you confirm

By accepting, you confirm that each of the following is true when you accept and
will remain true for as long as you use the Number:

**3.1 Authority.** The person accepting is authorised to bind you, and the
details you have given about your business, its regulated status and its
intended use of the Number are accurate.

**3.2 Licences and permissions.** You hold every licence, registration,
authorisation and regulatory permission required to send the traffic you intend
to send, in every jurisdiction where it originates or terminates.

**3.3 Consent.** You hold a lawful basis and, where required, the recorded
consent of each person you call. You will stop calling a person who withdraws
consent or asks not to be called, and you will honour that request across your
own systems, not only on the Number.

**3.4 Caller identity.** Any caller line identity, presentation number or
display name you present is one you are entitled to use. You will not spoof,
disguise or misrepresent the origin of a call, and you will comply with the CLI
rules of each jurisdiction you call into.

**3.5 Conduct of calls.** You will observe local rules on permitted calling
hours, call frequency, abandoned-call rates, opt-out mechanisms and
do-not-call registers.

**3.6 Recording.** Where you record calls, you will give any notice and obtain
any consent that local law requires, and you will handle recordings in
accordance with applicable data protection law. Telroi provides announcement and
consent-capture features; deciding when to use them is yours.

**3.7 AI-initiated calls.** Where an AI agent originates or answers calls on the
Number, you will disclose that the caller is an automated system where local law
requires it, and you will provide a route to a human on request.

**3.8 Your end users.** If you resell, sublicense or otherwise allow another
party to send traffic over the Number, you remain responsible for their conduct
as though it were your own, and you will impose obligations on them at least as
strict as those in this section.

## 4. Indemnity

**4.1** You will indemnify Telroi, its affiliates, and its officers, employees
and carriers against all claims, demands, proceedings, fines, regulatory
penalties, carrier charges, blocking or remediation costs, reasonable legal
costs and other losses arising from or connected with:

- your use of the Number, or use by anyone you permit to use it;
- any breach of the confirmations in section 3;
- any allegation that traffic on the Number was unlawful, unsolicited,
  fraudulent, deceptive or misleading; or
- any claim by a person you called, or by a regulator or carrier acting in
  relation to such a person.

**4.2** This applies whether the claim is brought against Telroi directly or
passed to Telroi by a carrier or upstream provider.

**4.3** Telroi will notify you promptly of any claim covered by this section,
will not settle it without consulting you, and will give you reasonable
assistance in responding at your cost.

**4.4** This indemnity survives the release of the Number and the termination of
your account.

## 5. What Telroi may do

**5.1 Decline or suspend.** Telroi may decline to provision a number, or suspend
or withdraw one already provisioned, where it reasonably believes traffic
breaches this Indemnity, where a carrier or regulator requires it, or where
traffic presents a risk to the network or to the people being called. Where
circumstances allow, Telroi will give notice first.

**5.2 Disclose.** Telroi may disclose your identity, your acceptance of this
Indemnity and records of traffic on the Number to a carrier, regulator, law
enforcement agency or court where required to do so or where necessary to answer
a complaint about the Number.

**5.3 Recover costs.** Telroi may set off against your wallet, or invoice you
for, any fine, carrier charge or remediation cost it incurs as a result of your
traffic.

## 6. Records

**6.1** Telroi retains your acceptance, the version of this document you
accepted, the identity and authority evidence supporting it, and the number to
which it relates, for the life of the Number and for **seven (7) years** after
it is released.

**6.2** These records are held to meet legal and carrier obligations and to
answer regulatory enquiries. They are not used for marketing. Because they exist
to meet a legal obligation, a request to erase them may be refused for as long
as that obligation applies.

**6.3** You may obtain a copy of your acceptance at any time from your workspace
or by contacting compliance@telroi.ai.

## 7. What this does not do

**7.1** This Indemnity does not transfer to Telroi any responsibility for the
content, timing, targeting, frequency or lawfulness of your traffic. Those
remain yours.

**7.2** Telroi does not review, approve or endorse your traffic, and provisioning
a number is not a representation that your intended use is lawful.

**7.3** This Indemnity does not make Telroi the controller of personal data you
process using the Number. Where Telroi processes personal data on your behalf, it
does so as a processor under the Telroi Data Processing Addendum.

**7.4** Nothing here limits any right or remedy Telroi has under the Terms of
Use or at law.

## 8. Changes

**8.1** Telroi may issue a new version of this Indemnity. A new version applies
to numbers provisioned after it takes effect. For numbers already provisioned,
Telroi will give at least thirty (30) days' notice before requiring acceptance
of a new version, and may suspend the number if it is not accepted within that
period.

**8.2** Your existing acceptance remains valid against the version you accepted,
and that version remains on record.
